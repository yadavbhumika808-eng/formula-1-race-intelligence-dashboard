# Tableau Dashboard Plan

## Worksheets
1. Driver Wins by Season — driver bars; season filter.
2. Driver Points Trend — points by season and driver.
3. Constructor Performance — team points/wins by season.
4. Circuit Overview — circuit type, country and circuit length.
5. Qualifying vs Race Finish — qualifying position vs finishing position.
6. DNF Reliability — DNF counts/reasons by driver and team.
7. Pole Position vs Race Wins — compare poles and wins.
8. Lap Pace and Sector Analysis — lap and sector times by driver/circuit.
9. Tyre Strategy / Pit Stop Analysis — tyre compound, pit stops and average pit-stop time.

## Dashboard filters
Season, Driver, Constructor, Country, Circuit Type, Weather, DNF Reason, Tyre Compound.

## Suggested story points
- Season and driver performance
- Constructor consistency
- Reliability and DNF patterns
- Qualifying pace versus race outcomes
- Tyre and pit-stop strategy

Only state findings supported by the actual charts. Validate the dataset and explain missing or inconsistent data.
